Intensive English & IELTS Preparation

For students who want faster measurable progress, or a specific goal such as an IELTS score, two of our partner schools offer an intensive track alongside their general English course.

Two Different Intensive Options

Intensive English at Stafford House & The Worthgate School, Canterbury

23 hours of English a week (versus the usual 15), for students aged 14-17 who want the fastest possible progress over a short stay. Runs alongside the same excursion and evening-activity programme as the Classic Course.

IELTS Preparation at Bosworth Independent School, Northampton

A focused option for older students who need a specific IELTS band score, for university entry or another formal purpose, taught alongside Bosworth's general and STEM English courses.

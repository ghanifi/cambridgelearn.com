    Intensive English & IELTS Preparation

    For students who want faster measurable progress, or a specific goal such as an IELTS score, two of our schools offer an intensive track alongside their general English course.

    Two Different Intensive Options

    Intensive English at Stafford House & The Worthgate School, Canterbury

    23 hours of English a week (versus the usual 15), for students aged 14-17 who want the fastest possible progress over a short stay. Runs alongside the same excursion and evening-activity programme as the Classic Course.

    IELTS Preparation at Bosworth Independent School, Northampton

    A focused option for older students who need a specific IELTS band score, for university entry or another formal purpose, taught alongside Bosworth's general and STEM English courses.

    Guide

    Intensive English and IELTS Summer Courses in the UK

    An intensive course is for students who want measurable progress in a short stay, or who have a specific goal such as an IELTS score for university entry. Our two intensive options are taught alongside the normal summer programme, so students still get the excursions, evening activities and residential experience.

    Intensive English in Canterbury: 23 hours a week

    At Stafford House & The Worthgate School in Canterbury, Intensive English gives 23 hours of lessons a week instead of the usual 15, for students aged 14-17. The extra hours make the biggest difference on short stays.

    IELTS Preparation in Northampton

    At Bosworth Independent School in Northampton, IELTS Preparation is aimed at older students who need a band score for university or another formal purpose. It is taught alongside the school's General English and STEM courses.

    Intensive or general?

    • Choose intensive if your child already has a clear goal or exam date
    • Choose General English for a first stay abroad or for students who want more time for activities
    • Ask us to check your child's current level before you decide

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How many hours a week is an intensive English course?

    23 hours a week on Intensive English in Canterbury, compared with 15 hours on the General English course.

    Do you offer an intensive IELTS course?

    Yes. Bosworth Independent School in Northampton offers IELTS Preparation for students who need a specific band score.

    Who is intensive English suitable for?

    Students aged 14-17 who want faster progress over a short stay or have a specific exam goal.

    Do intensive students still join excursions?

    Yes. Intensive students follow the same excursion and evening activity programme as the Classic Course.

    How much does an intensive course cost?

    It depends on the school, accommodation and number of weeks. Ask us for current prices.

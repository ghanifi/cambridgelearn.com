    Canterbury

    English Summer School in Canterbury

    A compact, walkable cathedral city on England's south coast doorstep, home to Stafford House Canterbury and The Worthgate School.

    Why Canterbury

    What Makes This Location Different

    • Canterbury Cathedral is the city's centrepiece and a short walk from both campuses, students get a genuine sense of English history without needing to travel.
    • The coast is close: Brighton and the Kent coastline are realistic weekend or excursion destinations, alongside London and Cambridge as included full-day trips.
    • Both residential and homestay accommodation are offered here, more flexibility than most of our other UK locations, plus an Intensive English option (23 hours a week) for students who want faster progress.

    Getting There

    Travel & Arrival

    Canterbury is about an hour by train from London, and within reach of the Eurostar terminal at Ashford. Homestay students are collected by their host family; residential students are met by the school's own arrival team.

    Guide

    English Language School in Canterbury: Courses and Options

    Canterbury is a historic cathedral city in Kent, within easy reach of London, and our Canterbury school offers the widest choice of courses of any of our locations. Classes run across two campuses: Stafford House Canterbury and The Worthgate School, a boarding school founded in 1952.

    Courses available in Canterbury

    • Classic Course: 15 hours of General English a week
    • Intensive English: 23 hours a week for faster progress
    • Classic Plus: Basketball
    • Medicine and Model United Nations courses

    Residence or host family

    Canterbury is one of only two of our locations that offers homestay. Students can live in residential rooms with 1-4 beds, all with private bathrooms, or with a local host family in a shared room from age 12. Ages run from 11 to 18 depending on the course and accommodation.

    Excursions

    One full-day excursion a week, for example to London, Brighton or Cambridge, plus local visits to Leeds Castle, Dover Castle or Canterbury Cathedral. Oxford and Thorpe Park are available as optional extras.

    Want more hours of English? See our Intensive English and IELTS courses, or compare residential and homestay accommodation.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is there an intensive English course in Canterbury?

    Yes. Intensive English in Canterbury gives 23 hours of lessons a week instead of the usual 15, with the same excursions and evening activities.

    Can students stay with a host family in Canterbury?

    Yes, from age 12, in a shared room with breakfast and dinner provided by the family. Residential rooms with private bathrooms are also available.

    What ages does the Canterbury school accept?

    From 11 to 18, depending on the course and type of accommodation. We confirm the exact age band for the course you choose.

    How far is Canterbury from London?

    Canterbury is within easy reach of London, and a full-day trip to London is one of the regular weekly excursions.

    How much does a summer course in Canterbury cost?

    It depends on the course, accommodation and number of weeks. Ask us for current prices for your dates.

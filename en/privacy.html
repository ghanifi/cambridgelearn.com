Who we are

Cambridge Learn ("we", "us") is a student placement agency operating from offices in London, United Kingdom and Antalya, Türkiye. Registered company name and number: [VERIFY — legal entity name and company registration number].

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number (if given), and the content of your message, which may include your child's age and the schools you're interested in.

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this website. We use Google Fonts, served from Google's servers, to display the site's typefaces; loading a page therefore sends your device's IP address to Google. We do not set any tracking cookies of our own.

How enquiry forms are handled

Form submissions on this site are sent directly to an internal team messaging channel so an advisor can respond quickly. This means your message is processed by our messaging provider as well as by us; it is not stored in a public or customer-facing database.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the information you asked for.
  • To match you with an appropriate partner school and share relevant details with that school where you ask us to.
  • To meet legal or safeguarding obligations connected with placing a minor at a partner school.

Children's information

Our service concerns arranging courses for students who are, in most cases, under 18. Enquiry forms are intended to be completed by a parent or guardian, not directly by a child. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us without appropriate parental involvement, please contact us and we will address it.

Who we share data with

We share the minimum necessary information with the specific partner school you enquire about, so they can confirm availability. We do not sell personal data. We may share information with UK or Turkish authorities where legally required, for example for safeguarding or visa purposes.

Before You Apply

UK Student Visa — What You'll Need

Every application is reviewed individually with you, but in general two groups of documents are required:

  • Student Documents

    Passport, biometric photos, student certificate, family registration record, a UK travel itinerary, and a signed sponsor letter from a parent or guardian.

  • Sponsor's Financial Documents

    Proof of income and bank statements from the sponsoring parent — the exact documents depend on whether they're employed, self-employed, or retired.

We'll send you the exact checklist

Requirements vary by nationality and by each parent's employment status. Once you enquire, we send a personalised document checklist and check everything before it's submitted.

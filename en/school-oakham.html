Oakham School

  • Oakham
  • Ages 8-17
  • Residential

Founded in 1584 in a countryside market town close to Cambridge, with extensive on-site sport and leisure facilities including an indoor swimming pool, sports hall and tennis courts. Residential accommodation is onsite in small boarding houses, mostly singles, twins and small dormitories.

Accommodation

Residential accommodation onsite in small boarding houses, mostly single, twin or small-dormitory rooms with shared bathrooms. Most houses have their own common room; laundry facilities and WiFi are provided.

Excursions

One full-day excursion a week (London, Cambridge, Warwick Castle) and two half-day excursions (Stamford, Rutland Water, Peterborough among them). Optional excursions include Oxford, Birmingham and Alton Towers.

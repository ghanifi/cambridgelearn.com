    Guildhouse School London

    • London
    • Ages 12-18
    • Residential Ensuite

    A Georgian townhouse school in the heart of Bloomsbury, central London, with travel cards included for the whole stay. Ensuite accommodation at Premier House (a few steps from school) or Dinwiddy House (two stops from King's Cross), both with communal lounges and flat-screen TVs.

    Accommodation

    Ensuite accommodation at Premier House (single and twin rooms, a few steps from school) or Dinwiddy House (ensuite rooms near King's Cross). Both have stylish communal areas with sofas, flat-screen TVs and a pool table. Homestay is also available for group bookings.

    Excursions

    One full-day excursion a week (Cambridge, Brighton, Canterbury) plus five included sightseeing visits around London itself, more than any of our other UK schools, covering places like the British Museum, the Science Museum and Covent Garden.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Where is Guildhouse School London?

    In a Georgian townhouse in Bloomsbury, central London, within walking distance of the British Museum.

    What ages does Guildhouse accept?

    Individual students aged 14-17 and groups aged 12-18. 18-year-olds are accepted only in groups whose students all return to secondary education the following September.

    Where do students stay?

    In ensuite rooms at Premier House, a few steps from the school, or Dinwiddy House near King's Cross. Homestay is also available for group bookings.

    Is London transport included?

    Yes. Travel cards are included for the whole stay.

    Which courses can students choose?

    London Explorer, London Academic Explorer, Bloomberg Business Advantage, STEM & English for Science, and Branding & Advertising.

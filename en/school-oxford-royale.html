    Oxford Royale — University of Oxford

    • Oxford
    • Ages 13-18
    • Residential (College)

    An academic summer school on campus at the University of Oxford, founded in 1096, the oldest university in the English-speaking world. Students stay in real Oxford college accommodation and can wander the cobbled streets, go punting on the river, or explore the Bodleian Library between classes. This is a different product to our other UK schools: an academic-enrichment course rather than a general English course, aimed at students who already have a solid level of English.

    Accommodation

    Real Oxford college accommodation at Balliol College, Queen's College, Keble College, St Hugh's College or St Peter's College, the largest and widest selection of college accommodation of any Oxford summer school.

    Excursions

    Off-campus during free time, students can go punting, tour the Bodleian Library, or visit the Divinity School and Christ Church's Great Hall (used as filming locations for the Harry Potter films).

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is Oxford Royale part of the University of Oxford?

    Oxford Royale runs its summer courses on the University of Oxford's campus, and students live in Oxford colleges: Balliol, Queen's, Keble, St Hugh's or St Peter's.

    Is it an English language course?

    Mostly not. It is an academic summer school with 13 subject areas taught in English, such as Medicine, Law & Politics and Engineering. English as a Foreign Language is one of the options.

    What ages can attend Oxford Royale?

    Students aged 13-18, taught in two age groups: 13-15 and 16-18.

    How long are the courses?

    2, 4 or 6 weeks. A 2-week course includes about 40 hours of academic teaching. Recent intakes have usually started in early July.

    What happens in the evenings?

    Dinner ranges from relaxed suppers to formal banquets, followed by quiz nights, debating challenges, silent discos and other events.

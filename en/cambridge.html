Cambridge

English Summer School in Cambridge

The city that gave our agency its name: an academic city built around King's and Trinity Colleges, the River Cam, and eight centuries of university history.

Why Cambridge

What Makes This Location Different

  • CATS Cambridge sits a short walk from King's and Trinity Colleges along the River Cam, punting and a city walking tour are built into the induction week.
  • The city is compact and walkable, students can explore beyond the school day without a long journey, one of the reasons this location suits slightly younger individual students as well as groups.
  • London is under an hour away by train, making it a realistic full-day excursion rather than an overnight trip.

Getting There

Travel & Arrival

Cambridge is roughly 50 minutes by train from London King's Cross, and around an hour and a half from Stansted Airport by road. Group arrivals are coordinated with the school; individual students are met according to the arrangements confirmed at booking.
